# Squarefree coefficients for infinite polynomial Cunningham chains

Fix a positive integer `r` and a cutoff `B`. Infinitely many squarefree integers `m` have exactly `r` distinct prime divisors, all exceeding `B`, and give an infinite first-kind polynomial Cunningham chain from `mX+1`. For each such coefficient, every iterate of `f ↦ Xf+1` is irreducible over ℚ. Jones gives the prime-coefficient family in his concluding remarks. Here the number of prime factors can be prescribed. [Main declaration](CunninghamSquarefreeLinearChains.lean) · [Jones, Corollary 1.6 and §4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1104.1579) · [Prime-coefficient proof](InfinitePolynomialChains.md)

The reciprocal of the iterate at index `j−1` is `Pⱼ = Xʲ + ⋯ + X + m`. A nontrivial monic integral factorization of this polynomial splits the constant term as `m = uv` with both factors greater than one. Two necessary congruences are `u^(j+1) ≡ 1 mod (m−1)` and `u^(2j) ≡ 1 mod v`. If `u` is odd and differs from one modulo a power of two dividing `m−1`, its order there is even, so `j+1` is even. If also `u² ≡ −1 mod p` for some odd prime dividing `v`, then `j` must be even. Either orientation of a split can give this contradiction. [Parity lemmas](CunninghamJonesSplitParity.lean) · [Irreducibility criterion](CunninghamJonesSplitCriterion.lean)

Write `r = n+1` and choose a power of two `W > 5ⁿ`. Choose increasing primes `q₀,…,qₙ` above the cutoff so that the first `n` are 5 modulo `W`, their full product is 1 modulo `W`, and all are 1 modulo 4. Modulo each `qᵢ`, its immediate successor squares to −1 and every later prime is 1. At each step the Chinese remainder theorem specifies a reduced residue class and Dirichlet’s theorem supplies an arbitrarily large prime in it. [Prime construction](CunninghamJonesPrimeConstruction.lean)

For a nontrivial split of the primes, take the side excluding `q₀` and let `qₜ` be its first member. Its product squares to −1 modulo `qₜ₋₁`, which belongs to the opposite side. The side excluding the last prime has product congruent to `5ˡ` for some `1 ≤ l ≤ n`, strictly between 1 and `W`. Since the full product is 1 modulo `W`, both sides differ from 1 there. This supplies both congruence obstructions in every split and every degree. Gauss’s lemma and polynomial reversal give the result for the original iterates. [Split argument](CunninghamJonesPrimePatterns.lean) · [Passage to iterates](CunninghamSquarefreeLinearChains.lean)

For example, `m = 65 = 5·13` meets the criterion with `W = 8`. Squarefreeness alone is insufficient. At `m = 21`, the third polynomial factors as `21X³+X²+X+1 = (3X+1)(7X²−2X+1)`.

The pinned DeepMind statements concern integer prime seeds and exact termination under `s ↦ 2s ± 1`. Their fixed-length infinitude remains open. Jones’s Conjecture 4.1 concerns a different family of specified coefficients and degrees. The present theorem is unconditional and makes an existence claim about polynomial chains.
