# When paired Cunningham prefixes can stop at prescribed composites

Start the first-kind recurrence at $n$ and the second-kind recurrence at $n+h$, with $h\ge0$. Fix positive prefix lengths $k,l$. Test each prime $q\le k+l$ for a residue $x\bmod q$ such that
$$
2^i(x+1)\ne1\quad(0\le i<k),\qquad
2^j(x+h-1)\ne-1\quad(0\le j<l)
$$
in $\mathbb F_q$. The residue may depend on $q$. The finite test is necessary and sufficient for each of the following conclusions, with the distribution input indicated in the second one. [Local test](CunninghamPairedLocalTest.lean) · [Equivalences](CunninghamPairedRoughStops.lean)

- **Unconditional.** There are distinct fixed primes $Q<R$ such that, for every cutoff $B$, infinitely many seeds $n\ge2$ have all $k+l$ prefix terms free of prime factors $\le B$. Each term has at most $324(k+l)+33$ prime factors counted with multiplicity. The two immediately following terms are composite and divisible by $Q$ and $R$, respectively.
- **Prime first seed.** The same test works with $n$ prime and the bound $1152(k+l)+117$. The Lean statement takes classical Bombieri–Vinogradov as an explicit hypothesis. The second seed $n+h$ is among the terms with bounded factor count. [Prime-supported affine sieve](CunninghamPrimeAffineRealization.lean) · [Pinned distribution input and external proof adapter](PrimeSeedAlmostPrimes.md)

## Which gaps work at every length?

Let $S$ be the primes at which 2 is a primitive root. The two prefixes can be realized at **every** pair of positive lengths exactly when $h$ is even and $h\equiv2\bmod q$ for every $q\in S$. In particular $h=2$ works unconditionally for unrestricted starts. The assertion that 2 is the only such gap is equivalent to the infinitude part of Artin’s conjecture at 2. The earlier equivalence concerned local congruences alone. The present equivalence includes actual bounded-almost-prime terms and two prescribed composite successors. [All-length theorem](CunninghamPairedPrimitiveThreshold.lean) · [Earlier local criterion](PairedGaps.md)

There is also an equivalence involving **finite** runs. For each $q\in S$ there are infinitely many even gaps for which the test first fails at common length $q-1$. It passes at $q-2$, and divisibility by $q$ obstructs length $q-1$ for every starting seed. Choose $h\equiv-2\bmod q$ and $h\equiv2$ modulo all other primes through $2q$. At $q$, the residue $x=1$ survives exactly while $2^{i+1}\ne1$. At the other tested primes $x=-1$ works. Conversely, if $S$ is finite, passing a common length larger than every member of $S$ forces the all-length gap congruences. Thus arbitrarily late finite failures for realizable pairs exist exactly when $S$ is infinite. This equivalence for unrestricted starts uses no unproved number-theoretic hypothesis. [Sharp thresholds and Artin equivalence](CunninghamPairedPrimitiveThreshold.lean)

For example, gap 8 passes at common length three and fails at four. Modulo 5 the sole surviving seed residue through three positions is 1. Extending both prefixes one step covers all five residues. This rules out a pair of prime prefixes of length four from any first seed above 5. [Kernel-checked gap-eight example and divisibility obstruction](CunninghamPairedPrimitiveThreshold.lean)

## Why the stopping terms can be imposed

A prime larger than $k+l$ is odd and each of the $k+l$ dyadic forms excludes one residue at most. For the stopping terms, choose primes at which their roots avoid all earlier roots. Within each chain the rational roots are distinct. A coincidence across signs at indices $i,j$ requires $h=2-2^{-i}-2^{-j}$. For an even gap and at least one positive index this is impossible. More generally, if $A,C$ specify the terminal form by its slope and value at two, and $a_i,c_i$ specify the prefix forms, each nonzero determinant $a_i C-A c_i$ excludes only finitely many candidate stopping primes. This lets us choose the two primes successively. The first seed is itself a prefix term, so every locally safe residue is a unit. [Separation of the terminal roots](CunninghamPairedBoundaryRoots.lean) · [Choice of the two primes](CunninghamPairedLocalTest.lean)

For unrestricted seeds, the Chinese remainder theorem imposes the chosen residues before applying the elementary Brun lower sieve. For prime seeds the notebook’s Bombieri–Vinogradov sieve preserves all the specified small-prime residues. Both constructions yield arbitrarily large seeds, so the two stopping divisors are proper. [Affine realizations](CunninghamPrimeAffineRealization.lean) · [Elementary lower sieve](CunninghamBrunLowerSieve.lean) · [Prime-parameter sieve](CunninghamPrimeSieveCount.lean)

## Scope and checks

The DeepMind predicates require every term in the prefix to be prime and the next term to be nonprime. Here each side stops at its specified composite but earlier composites are allowed. The two Jones infinitude questions for natural prime chains remain open. Dickson’s conjecture supplies genuinely prime paired prefixes at gap two in the earlier notebook result.
