# An explicit finite prefix for both balanced Jones chains

Fix an integer a ≥ 2. Start at T₀ = a(X−1) and iterate Tₙ₊₁ = XTₙ + ε for ε = ±1. Both Tₙ are irreducible over ℚ once n ≥ 12·4^(1+5a²+(a+1)²). Consequently, simultaneous irreducibility at every index is equivalent to irreducibility of both chains at every earlier index. This supplies a numerical cutoff in the square-coefficient minus case left open by the earlier notebook proof. The bound is sufficient and can be very large. [Finite-prefix theorem](CunninghamAllBalancedCutoff.lean) · [Earlier qualitative result](AllBalancedEventual.md)

More precisely, for a = b² with b ≥ 2 put W = 1+5a²+(a−1)². The minus iterate is irreducible whenever n+2 > 6(3ᵂ+1). For example a = 4 gives W = 90, while n = 3 actually is exceptional. For the plus side and the nonsquare minus side the existing endpoint theorem gives 12·4ᵂ with W = 1+5a²+(a+ε)². [Closed endpoint weights](CunninghamBalancedEndpointWeight.lean) · [Earlier endpoint theorem](EventualBalancedChains.md)

## Moving a witness to a larger degree

Let N = n+2 and suppose a reflected-product witness has t nonzero coefficients at positions x₁,…,xₜ. Apart from these positions, the sparse Jones polynomial has four marked slots at 0, N, N−1 and N−2. The earlier two-degree comparison shows that this degree cannot be exceptional if one can move to a larger degree while preserving every equality of differences between marked positions. A prior notebook result used four bins to compress the support of a different Jones polynomial. Here three residues per witness slot suffice, and the upper three slots must move together. [Two-degree comparison](CunninghamSquareGapComparison.lean) · [Earlier four-bin compression](JonesLargeDegree.md)

Assume N > 6(3ᵗ+1). For each multiplier j in 3,6,…,3(3ᵗ+1), record ⌊jxᵢ/N⌋ modulo 3 at each witness slot. There are at most 3ᵗ records, so two multipliers r < s agree. Move a position x by v(x) = ⌊sx/N⌋−⌊rx/N⌋. The displacement is between 0 and s−r. Zero stays fixed. The three upper slots move by s−r because 2s < N and both multipliers are positive. All marked displacements are divisible by three. [Displacement construction](CunninghamThreeColorStretch.lean)

For x+y = z+w, the two sums of floors at any fixed multiplier differ by at most one. Subtracting the two multipliers gives an imbalance between −2 and 2. For marked positions it is divisible by three, so it vanishes. This preserves every required equality of differences at the larger degree. Reducing these floor records modulo two would lose this implication. At N = 7 with multipliers 2 and 5, the displacements at 1, 3 and 5 are 0, 2 and 2. All are even, yet 1+5 = 3+3 has displacements summing to 2 and 4. [Exceptional-degree bound](CunninghamEffectiveSquareComparison.lean)

The reflected-product identity bounds t by W through the sum of squared coefficients. The square-endpoint comparison and reciprocal-root exclusion therefore prove irreducibility beyond the displayed cutoff. Effective large-gap theorems of Schinzel and of Filaseta, Ford and Konyagin already apply to the mathematical family. The Lean argument here gives the stated specialized bound. Jones's polynomial discussion is in §4. The canonical DeepMind predicates concern natural prime seeds of prescribed exact chain length for q ↦ 2q ± 1. Both infinitude questions remain open.
