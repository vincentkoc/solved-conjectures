# Exact second-kind Cunningham chains under Dickson

`CunninghamChain.infinitely_many_secondKind_chains_of_primeTuples` proves that Dickson's prime-tuples conjecture implies infinitely many seeds of each positive exact length for the recurrence `p ↦ 2p − 1`. Jones records this implication in §1. The unconditional infinitude question remains open. [Jones, *Polynomial Cunningham Chains*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1104.1579) · [Lean proof](CunninghamSecondKindDickson.lean)

Both sides of the implication are taken directly from the pinned upstream declarations with `type_of%`. In particular, the conclusion uses `IsSecondKindChainOfLength`, including the nonprime term at index `k`. The upstream Dickson hypothesis requires at least two forms and permits repetition. [Cunningham definitions and conjecture](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/blob/f606af160664c5a10952e9a957b100a1cd45bbeb/FormalConjectures/Arxiv/1104.1579/CunninghamChain.lean) · [Dickson hypothesis](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/blob/f606af160664c5a10952e9a957b100a1cd45bbeb/FormalConjectures/Paper/PrimeTuples.lean)

For a seed `x + 1`, term `i` equals `2^i x + 1`. In particular the seed 1 stays at 1. Choose a prime `q = 2^k b + 1` with `b > 0` and take seeds `q n + b + 1`. Modulo `q`, every earlier term has a representative strictly between zero and `q`. For any other prime `p`, choose `n` with `q n + b ≡ 0 (mod p)`. All terms are then 1 modulo `p`. Dickson gives infinitely many parameters with prime prefix. For each positive such parameter the next term is exactly `q (2^k n + 1)`, with both factors greater than one.

The upstream statement has a closed issue and merged statement PR. OMA splits off the unconditional length-one case, with two PRs proving that case. Searches by title, declaration, arXiv identifier and OEIS A181715 found no matching all-length conditional proof in DeepMind, OMA or the LTS solved list.
