# Infinitely many prefixes at one fixed length

Fix one positive length `k` and either Cunningham recurrence. Infinitely many seeds give prime terms at indices `0,…,k−1` if and only if infinitely many seeds give **exact** length `k`, including a nonprime term at index `k`. In particular, the first-kind case at `k = 2` is equivalent to infinitude of Sophie Germain primes. Both infinitude assertions at length two remain open. [Lean proof](CunninghamFixedLengthPrefixes.lean) · [Jones, §1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1104.1579#page=2)

The length-two declaration `CunninghamChain.firstKind_exact_two_iff_safe_primes` uses the original `IsFirstKindChainOfLength` and the full type of `Dickson.infinite_safe_primes`. The latter asks for infinitely many primes `p` with `2p+1` prime. It imposes no condition on `4p+3`. The equivalence needs neither Dickson’s conjecture nor an assumption about any other length. [Upstream Cunningham predicates](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/blob/f606af160664c5a10952e9a957b100a1cd45bbeb/FormalConjectures/Arxiv/1104.1579/CunninghamChain.lean) · [upstream Sophie Germain statement](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/blob/f606af160664c5a10952e9a957b100a1cd45bbeb/FormalConjectures/Wikipedia/Dickson.lean#L59-L65)

Choose a prefix seed `p` above any bound. Its chain eventually reaches a nonprime. If the prefix already ends immediately before a nonprime, keep its seed. Otherwise advance the seed by one term. The new prefix still has `k` primes, its seed remains above the bound, and the known nonprime is one step closer. This gives an exact seed above every bound. Only the previous notebook’s termination theorem is needed from its work on unbounded lengths. [Termination proof](CunninghamUnboundedPrefixes.lean)

**Restricted seeds.** The argument works inside any set `S` such that a prime in `S` stays in `S` whenever its successor is prime. In particular it works for `d ∣ p+1` in the first kind and `d ∣ p−1` in the second kind, since a step doubles the shifted quantity. An arbitrary single residue class can lose the suffix. For example the first-kind chain starting at 5 is `5, 11, 23, 47, 95`, with 95 composite. At length two the exact suffix starts at 23, changing the seed from 1 to 3 modulo 4.

The file prints the axiom reports for both invariant-set theorems, both unrestricted equivalences, the paired divisibility-class corollary and the Sophie Germain equivalence. Each uses only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` in Lean 4.33.1. The [notebook replay](../../README.md#replay) discovers the imported termination module.
