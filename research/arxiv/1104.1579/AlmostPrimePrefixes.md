# Almost-prime Cunningham prefixes of every length

Fix a length `k ≥ 1` and either Cunningham recurrence. There is a prime `q` such that, for **every** cutoff `B`, infinitely many seeds `p ≥ 2` satisfy both conditions below.

- Each of the first `k` terms has at most `324k + 33` prime factors counted with multiplicity, all greater than `B`.
- The term at index `k` is composite and divisible by `q`. The same `q` works at every cutoff.

The proof is unconditional. Jones’s exact-length predicates require primality at **every** index below `k`, including the seed. The theorem weakens that requirement and enforces the original composite-successor condition. It leaves infinitude of exact prime chains open for `k ≥ 2`. ## Admissible linear forms

The general theorem treats `k` forms `aᵢt + cᵢ` with positive natural slopes and nonnegative intercepts. Its hypothesis is local. For each prime there must be a parameter at which the prime divides none of the forms. For every cutoff there are then infinitely many parameters for which **each** form has the factor bound above and avoids primes through the cutoff. The constant depends only on the number of forms. Classical weighted sieves give substantially sharper bounds on the total number of factors. The estimate here comes from a direct formalization of Brun's lower sieve. [Ford’s sieve notes](https://ford126.web.illinois.edu/sieve2023.pdf) · [Maynard](https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.4610) · [Lewulis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.09432)

Use the Chinese remainder theorem once to avoid the small primes, including all prime factors of the slopes. Each larger prime excludes at most `k` residue classes. Group primes into bands `(w,w²]`. The bound on the sum of `(log q)/q` controls the sum of the forbidden densities in a band by `4k`. Use an even truncation of inclusion–exclusion on each band. Subtracting its first omitted odd intersection count times the other even truncations gives a lower bound for the indicator that all bands are avoided. Take higher truncation orders on smaller primes so the relative losses sum to at most one half. Counting the selected intersections by the Chinese remainder theorem controls the incomplete-period error. This gives parameters for which every form is at most `Z^(324k+33)` and has no prime factor through `Z`, with `Z` arbitrarily large. [Lower sieve](CunninghamBrunLowerSieve.lean) · [Intersection counts](CunninghamBrunSieve.lean) · [Prime sum estimate](CunninghamSelbergSieve.lean)

For the chain application, Dirichlet’s theorem gives a seed `v ≥ 2` for which the term at index `k` is prime. Write this prime as `q` and take seeds `qn + v`. Their successors at index `k` equal `q(2ᵏn + 1)`. At the prime `q`, the earlier terms avoid zero because their values at `n = 0` lie strictly between zero and `q`. At every other prime, choose the seed congruent to the fixed point of the recurrence, `−1` for the first kind and `1` for the second. This proves admissibility for the preceding `k` forms.
