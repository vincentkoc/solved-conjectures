# DeepMind proof notebook

These Lean files develop partial results for seven problems in [Formal Conjectures](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/tree/f606af160664c5a10952e9a957b100a1cd45bbeb). Codex assisted with the proofs. Each file prints its theorem’s axioms. The notes link the original question, prior work and the proof.

| [Green 33](green/NOTES.md) | Every basis satisfies [`2q ≤ k² + 2`](green/REFLECTION-RIGIDITY.md), with exact reflection symmetry at equality. A pointwise cap of `m + 4` implies `(2m + 2)q + 6 ≤ m k² + (m + 8)k`. Along every family with `q → ∞` and `k/√q → √2`, the [maximum ordered count tends to infinity](green/DIVERGENCE.md). All [nontrivial character sums](green/FOURIER-STABILITY.md) are then `o(√q)`. Residues above any fixed cutoff carry [at least `(2 − o(1))q` of the energy](green/TAIL-ENERGY.md). | Existence of a family attaining this density remains open. |
| [Green 33 · order-three quotient](green/ORDER-THREE.md) | If `3 ∣ q`, equality in `2q ≤ k² + 2` forces `4k − 7` to be a square. This raises the size bound by one at every `q = 2(15n + 5)² + 1`, with exact minimum 11 at `q = 51`. | The obstruction concerns exact finite equality. |
| [Green 33 · explicit cyclic bases](green/BEL-CONSTRUCTION.md) | The Bevan–Erskine–Lewis construction gives a basis of size `4q` modulo `6q(q − 2)` for every `q = 6n + 7`, attaining the limiting constant `√(8/3)`. | Green asks for the smaller constant `√2`. |
| [Green 33 · lifting a strict sum cover](green/STRICT-COVER-LIFT.md) | Twelve progressions give cyclic two-bases of size `12(Q+27720)` modulo `56Q²`, with limiting ratio `√(18/7)`. | Reaching `√2` remains open. |
| [Green 33 · distinct summands](green/STRICT-REDUCTION.md) | Green’s conjecture is equivalent to the infimum of `k/√N` over all finite cyclic strict sum covers being `√2`. An ordinary seed of size `k` gives a strict lift with normalized overhead at most `(2⌊√k⌋+3)/√N` relative to the seed modulus `N`. | The value of the infimum remains open. |
| [Green 33 · approximate cyclic covers](green/APPROXIMATE-COVERS.md) | Green 33 is equivalent to optimal-size seeds missing `o(M)` residues, or having `o(M)` excess unordered representations, with or without diagonal pairs. A seed missing `e` distinct-pair sums gives `M ≤ S(k+2√e)²`. | Constructing such a sequence remains open. |
| [Green 33 → Erdős 840](green/QUASI-SIDON-INTERVALS.md) | Green 33 implies that `√2` is a quasi-Sidon constant for integer intervals at every large `N`. The finite transfer preserves the strict cyclic extremal order as a lower bound for the number of distinct integer sums. | The `√2` lower bound is conditional on Green 33. |
| [Green 33 · Sidon quotients](green/SIDON-QUOTIENT-PROFILE.md) | At Green’s size, cyclic Sidon sets projected modulo half the order have ordered count proportions `1/4, 1/2, 1/4` at counts `0, 2, 4`. Ruzsa’s prime family attains the quarter-hole bound with unordered cap two. | Adding `o(√M)` labels cannot repair this class. Green 33 remains open. |
| [Erdős 864 → Green 33](green/ERDOS-864-EXTRACTION.md) | The conjectured interval upper bound forces every sufficiently large cyclic two-basis to have more than `√(2M) + 1/10` labels. The finite interval extraction is unconditional. | The additive gap is conditional and is compatible with Green’s relative error. |
| [Erdős 864 · reflection stability](erdos/ERDOS-864-WEIGHTED-CORE.md) | At the `√2` endpoint, deleting `o(√N)` labels leaves a reflected core. Attaining this endpoint is equivalent to interval Sidon sets of density `|B|/√L → 1` with `3B ∩ (B+e) = ∅` for `e ∈ {0,1}`. | Attainment and the conjectured upper constant `2/√3` remain open. |
| [Green 33 · translation overlap](green/TRANSLATE-OVERLAP.md) | At Green’s size and sumset density tending to one, every nonzero translation overlaps in `o(√M)` labels. The finite bound `b(b−1) ≤ 4E` has the best uniform factor for cyclic groups. | This excludes families with positive relative overlap. Existence remains open. |
| [Green 33 · prime-order obstruction](green/PRIME-MOMENTS.md) | At prime order `p = 2m² + 1`, an extremal basis of size `2m` with `m > 1` forces `4ⁱ ≡ −m (mod p)` for some `i < m`. Equality is impossible for `m ≡ 1 (mod 4)`, giving at least 19 elements modulo 163. | The obstruction concerns exact finite equality. |
| [Green 33 · higher moments](green/NEWTON-OBSTRUCTION.md) | At prime-order equality, the basis size belongs to the additive submonoid generated by the resonant moment degrees. Modulo `173203273`, every basis has at least `18613` elements, although `18612` passes the first moment test. | The exact minimum at this modulus is unknown here. |
| [Green 33 · stability under deletion](green/HEREDITARY-TAIL.md) | In any family at the proposed density, every subset of size at least `α√q`, for fixed `α > 1`, has diverging maximum ordered multiplicity. Its own energy above a fixed cutoff is at least `(2α² − 2 − o(1))q`, uniformly in the subset. | Existence of the ambient family remains open. |
| [Green 33 · small self-sumsets](green/PIECE-OBSTRUCTION.md) | If a basis family is covered by a fixed number `r ≥ 2` of pieces with total self-sumset size `o(q)`, its limiting normalized size is at least `√(2r/(r−1))`. The four-progression bound is sharp. | Bases without such covers remain eligible for Green’s constant. |
| [Green 33 · maximal cyclic orders](green/MAXIMAL-ORDERS.md) | Graham–Sloane’s strict and ordinary largest orders divided by `k²` converge for every size budget to the supremum of finite strict cover densities. | [Green 33 · undirected circulants](green/UNDIRECTED-ORDERS.md) | At fixed diameter two, the largest undirected cyclic order at exact degree `d`, divided by `d²`, converges along all degrees. Its limit is the supremum of explicit finite seed efficiencies and is at most the ordinary cyclic limit. | The value of the undirected limit remains open here. |
| [Green 54 · Gaussian convexity](green/GAUSSIAN-PROJECTIONS.md) | Every centered finite-dimensional 2-Lipschitz Gaussian image is a sum of two standard Gaussian vectors. Finite Langevin steps admit this representation, with a quantitative approximation estimate for strongly convex targets. | The positive DeepMind statement follows from Caffarelli’s contraction theorem. | [Cunningham chains](arxiv/NOTES.md) | Dickson’s prime-tuples conjecture implies infinitely many first-kind chains of every positive **exact** length. | [First-kind chains in residue classes](arxiv/1104.1579/FirstKindProgressions.md) | Under Dickson, a class contains infinitely many exact length-`k` seeds iff each of its first `k` terms is coprime to the modulus. | [Second-kind Cunningham chains](arxiv/1104.1579/CunninghamSecondKindDickson.md) | Under Dickson, infinitely many seeds have each positive exact length for the recurrence `p ↦ 2p − 1`. | Unconditional infinitude remains open. |

| [Fixed Cunningham lengths and Sophie Germain primes](arxiv/1104.1579/FixedLengthPrefixes.md) | At each fixed positive length and for either kind, infinitely many prime prefixes are equivalent to infinitely many exact chains. | [Cunningham chain length bound](arxiv/1104.1579/QuarterSeedBound.md) | For both kinds, an exact length `k` from seed `p` satisfies `4k < p` with exactly seven exceptions, all verified. | Infinitude at fixed length remains open. |
| [Cunningham chains under Artin’s conjecture](arxiv/1104.1579/ArtinLengthBound.md) | Positive relative density of base-two primitive-root primes implies `k ≤ C log p` for exact chains of either kind. | [Exact Cunningham chain counts](arxiv/1104.1579/BatemanHornCounts.md) | For each fixed `k > 0`, the two signs have the same positive Euler-product constant. | [Reciprocal sums for Cunningham chains](arxiv/1104.1579/BrunReciprocals.md) | For either kind, reciprocal seeds of exact length `k` form a summable series exactly when `k ≠ 1`. The prime-pair subseries converges unconditionally. | Infinitude of seeds of each exact length `k ≥ 2` remains open. |

| [Almost-prime Cunningham prefixes](arxiv/1104.1579/AlmostPrimePrefixes.md) | At every positive length and for both kinds, infinitely many prefixes have at most `324k+33` prime factors per term, all above any prescribed cutoff. | [Polynomial Cunningham chains of exact length](arxiv/1104.1579/PolynomialLengths.md) | Both signs have infinitely many quadratic seeds of every positive exact length over ℚ. ## Replay

Use Python 3.9 or newer and a Formal Conjectures checkout at `f606af160664c5a10952e9a957b100a1cd45bbeb` with its pinned Lean 4.33.1 dependencies. Set `FC` to the path of that checkout. Run the following from this repository’s root. The individual proof notes also use `$notebook` to locate their Lean files from `$FC`.

```bash
export notebook="$PWD/research"
runner="$PWD/scripts/replay-research"
(cd "${FC:?set FC to the Formal Conjectures checkout}" && lake env python3 "$runner")
```

The runner discovers every `.lean` file under `research/`, resolves its imports with Lean, and compiles notebook dependencies first. Use a unique module filename for each proof. Each file must print its theorem’s axioms. The runner checks the reports against `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`, then replays all compiled notebook modules with `leanchecker`. Compiled notebook files are temporary.

The [theta Fourier replay](arxiv/theta-sum/FOURIER.md#replay) first compiles its diagonal-module dependency.
