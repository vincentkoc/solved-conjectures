# The tetrahedral action and the sixth factorial collision

The smallest difference between two indices with equal factorial residues is six on a set of primes of ordinary density $55/3456$. The primes with no collision through gap six have density $77/3456$. Here both indices lie in $[1,p)$ and the denominator is $\pi(X)$. At $p=193$ the two gap-six pairs are $(82,88)$ and $(104,110)$, and all smaller gaps are absent. ### Why four vertices control six roots

Write $f_k(X)=X(X-1)\cdots(X-k+1)-1$. The cubic resolvent of $f_6$ has roots $a^2,b^2,c^2$ with $abc=17$, $a^2+b^2+c^2=35$ and $a^2b^2+a^2c^2+b^2c^2=259$. The four even-sign sums $a+b+c$, $a-b-c$, $-a+b-c$, $-a-b+c$ are the roots of $Q(X)=X^4-70X^2-136X+189$. The six roots of $f_6$ are $(5\pm a)/2,(5\pm b)/2,(5\pm c)/2$. An unordered pair of distinct vertices $x,y$ corresponds to the root $(10+x+y)/4$. This map is a bijection and commutes with all field automorphisms. The two root sets generate the same field. Its degree is 24, so every permutation of the four vertices occurs. [Root coordinates and field degree](Erdos478TetrahedralRootFields.lean) · [equivariant edge bijection](Erdos478TetrahedralEdgeCoordinates.lean) · [quartic action](Erdos478TetrahedralVertexAction.lean)

At the single regular prime $1439$, the polynomials $f_2,f_3,f_4,f_5,Q$ have respectively $2,0,4,2,2$ roots. One Frobenius lift acts trivially on the quadratic and quartic gap roots, by one three-cycle on each of the cubic and quintic gap roots, and by a transposition on the four vertices. Its cube fixes every lower root and still transposes two vertices. The lower pointwise stabilizer is normal, so its image contains every conjugate of the transposition and hence all of $S_4$. The proof gives the same criterion for an arbitrary finite symmetric action and any witnessing exponent. Consequently every attainable tuple of permutations on the gap-two through gap-five roots combines with **every** attainable permutation of the actual six gap-six roots. The combined splitting field has exact degree $11520\cdot24=276480$. The independence holds for every real observable of the sixth fixed-root count against every real weight depending only on the lower permutations.
