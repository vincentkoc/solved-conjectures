# Concentration of factorial collisions in proportional gap intervals

Fix $L>1$. Let $H(K)$ be any integer cutoff with $H(K)/K\to L$. For a prime $p$, count each upper endpoint $a$ once if some gap $\ell$ satisfies
$$
K<\ell\le H(K),\quad \ell\text{ prime},\quad \ell\equiv11\text{ or }17\pmod{21},
\qquad 1\le a-\ell<a<p,\quad (a-\ell)!\equiv a!\pmod p.
$$
Write $T(K,p)$ for this count and $A_p=\{n!\bmod p:1\le n<p\}$. For every $\eta,\varepsilon>0$, for all sufficiently large $K$, and then for all sufficiently large $X$, **more than a $1-\varepsilon$ proportion of the primes $p\le X$** satisfy both
$$
\left|\frac{T(K,p)\log K}{K}-\frac{L-1}{6}\right|<\eta,
\qquad |A_p|+T(K,p)\le p-1.
$$
Put $c=(L-1)/6$. More precisely, for every $A>c/\eta^2$, the exceptional proportion is less than $A\log K/K$ for all sufficiently large $K$ and then all sufficiently large $X$. The cutoff for $X$ may depend on $K$. Taking $H(K)=\lfloor LK\rfloor$ gives the fixed-ratio case. For example, take $L=2$. For every sufficiently large integer $K$ and all sufficiently large $X$ depending on $K$, **more than 99%** of primes up to $X$ have at least $\lceil K/(7\log K)\rceil$ distinct repeated upper endpoints from gaps in $(K,2K]$. The canonical factorial image misses at least that many nonzero residues. Here the exceptional proportion is less than $295\log K/K$, since $(1/6)/(1/6-1/7)^2=294$. The coefficient $1/7$ can be replaced by any fixed real $b<1/6$. The earlier dyadic result gives a one-sided $C2^j/j^3$ bound on a subsequence of dyadic bands. Removing proper prime powers costs at most $\psi(N)-\theta(N)=O(\sqrt N)$. The selected prime log-weight in $(K,H(K)]$, divided by $K$, therefore tends to $(L-1)/6$. Each log-weight in the interval lies between $\log K$ and $\log H(K)$, and their ratio tends to one. If $m_K$ is the number of selected prime gaps, this gives $m_K\log K/K\to(L-1)/6$. The argument applies to any selection with a von Mangoldt asymptotic and any integer upper cutoff with the stated ratio limit. For a fixed window, the ordinary-prime mean of $(T(K,p)-m_K)^2$ tends to $m_K$. This uses the earlier double-transitivity result for each selected gap and the mixed root moments for distinct prime gaps. Consequently $m_K(\log K/K)^2\to0$. To test tolerance $\eta$ around $c$, use the centered tolerance $d_K=\eta-|m_K\log K/K-c|$. For large $K$ it is positive and tends to $\eta$. Chebyshev’s inequality bounds the upper limiting exceptional proportion by $m_K(\log K/K)^2/d_K^2$. Dividing by $\log K/K$ gives the limiting bound $c/\eta^2$. Deleting all counted upper endpoints preserves a smaller representative of each factorial residue. DeepMind’s question asks for a limit of $|A_p|/p$ along all primes. The two cutoffs in the concentration theorem leave that limit open.
