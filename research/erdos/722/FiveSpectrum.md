## The four ingredients

Write $n=4u+1$. Start with a pairwise balanced design on $u$ points whose block sizes belong to $\{5,6,10,16\}$. Replace every point by a group of four and add a shared point. Inside a master block of size $s$, use a five-point design on $4s+1$ points. Deleting one point gives $s$ groups of four and one block through each pair from different groups. The master design determines the unique local filling for each such pair. Each group together with the shared point supplies one final block of five. [Uniform deletion and inflation](../Erdos722QuartetDeletion.lean)

The four local designs have orders $21,25,41,65$. Five columns over the fields of orders four and five supply the first two. In $\mathbb Z/41\mathbb Z$, the ordered differences within $\{1,10,16,18,37\}$ and $\{36,32,2,33,20\}$ represent each nonzero residue exactly once. Their translates give the third design. The fourth is the Hermitian unital of order four. ## Covering every large group count

Five full columns of size $t$ and a sixth column of size $r\le t$ give master blocks of sizes five and six. One may fill the columns separately or first adjoin a point shared by all columns. Thus for $a\in\{0,1\}$, master designs of orders $t+a$ and $r+a$ give one of order $5t+r+a$. The construction uses the evaluation directions $0,1,2,3,4$ modulo $t$ when $t\equiv1,5\pmod6$. The fields of orders nine and sixteen supply the two additional values of $t$. [Truncation and the two fillings](Erdos722FiveMaster.lean)

A bit at position $u$ records a proved master order. Starting at $0,1,5,6,10,16$, the certificate applies the two fillings for $1\le t\le4100$. Lean verifies that every $u\equiv0,1\pmod5$ in $[3200,20500)$ occurs. For $u\ge20500$, take $t=30\lfloor u/180\rfloor+31$ and $r=u-5t$. Both $t$ and $r$ are smaller admissible orders at least $3200$, and $r\le t$. Strong induction now covers the whole tail. Inflation gives the stated cutoff $4\cdot3200+1=12801$.
