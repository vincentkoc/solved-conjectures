# Integral designs in every rank

For every $0\le r\le k\le n$, there are **integer** weights on the $k$-subsets of an $n$-set, summing to one through every $r$-subset, exactly when all the binomial divisibilities in Erdős 722 hold. Nonnegative weights at index one give exactly the host's ordinary Steiner systems. The two conditions can differ even along an infinite family with growing $k$ and $n$. [Signed index one and the exact host predicate](Erdos722SteinerWeights.lean) · [Pinned Erdős 722](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/blob/f606af160664c5a10952e9a957b100a1cd45bbeb/FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems/722.lean)

Take any even $m\ge4$, put $k=2m-1$, and put $v=(k-2)m+2$. A signed $3$-design of index one exists at every one of these parameters. An ordinary $S(3,k,v)$ is impossible. The first pairs $(k,v)$ are $(7,22)$, $(11,56)$ and $(15,106)$. The required numbers of blocks through a fixed set of zero, one or two points are respectively $mv/2$, $mk/2$ and $m$. To see the obstruction, take a point of one block and a point outside it. Blocks through this pair must cover each of the other $k-1$ points in the original block. Two of those points in a single such block would give a triple covered twice. Thus the pair would need at least $k-1=2m-2>m$ blocks. Consequently, for every $k=4t+7$ with $t\ge0$, any valid rank-three order threshold at $k$ exceeds $(k-2)(k+1)/2+2$. The block size grows in this example. The host allows its order threshold to depend on the block size. [Infinite family](Erdos722SignedTripleFamily.lean) · [Exterior-point bound](Erdos722SignedSteinerObstruction.lean) · [Cameron, *Parallelisms of Complete Designs*, theorem 3A.4](https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511662102)

## Arbitrary integer demands

Let $V$ have $n\ge k+r$ points. Assign an arbitrary integer $y(A)$ to each $r$-subset. The theorem `signed_criterion` characterizes when $y(A)$ is the sum of integer weights on the $k$-blocks containing $A$. For each $I\subseteq V$ with $i=|I|\le r$, the criterion is
$$
\binom{k-i}{r-i}\ \bigm|\ \sum_{\substack{|A|=r\\I\subseteq A}}y(A).
$$
These are the classical integral design conditions of Graver–Jurkat and Wilson. The earlier notebook proof treated pair incidence in Wilson's rank-two existence theorem. The present proof allows arbitrary demand vectors in all ranks under the stated size bound. [Integral criterion](Erdos722IntegralDesignCriterion.lean) · [Graver and Jurkat](https://doi.org/10.1016/0097-3165(73)90037-X) · [Earlier rank-two construction](WilsonPairSpectrum.md)

At the boundary $n=k+r$, let $d(I)$ be the integer quotient in the display. Assign to each $k$-block $B$ the weight $\sum_{I\subseteq V\setminus B}(-1)^{|I|}d(I)$. On summing over all blocks through $A$, each $I$ disjoint from $A$ occurs $\binom{k-i}{r-i}$ times. This recovers the numerator. Interchanging the remaining sums gives the coefficient $\sum_{I\subseteq S\setminus A}(-1)^{|I|}$ on $y(S)$, equal to one for $S=A$ and zero otherwise. For larger $n$, first fill the rows containing a chosen point by reducing both ranks by one. Subtract that contribution and apply induction on the remaining point set. Double counting keeps the residual divisibilities intact. ## Nonnegative multiplicities and the size of the index

For a constant demand $\lambda\ge0$, the signed criterion extends to **all** $r\le k\le n$. Its tests are $\binom{k-i}{r-i}\mid\lambda\binom{n-i}{r-i}$ for every $i<r$. When $n\le k+r$, they are equivalent to $\binom{n-r}{k-r}\mid\lambda$. In this range constant natural weights on all $k$-blocks realize every admissible index. Inclusion–exclusion over the complement of one block proves the last divisibility. [Uniform indices](Erdos722SignedIndexSpectrum.lean) · [Small orders](Erdos722SmallOrderIndices.lean)

For fixed $n,r,k$ the same tests characterize all sufficiently large indices with **nonnegative integer multiplicities**, allowing repeated blocks. The complete design has index $C=\binom{n-r}{k-r}$. Admissibility is preserved on reducing an index modulo $C$. Choose signed weights for each admissible residue. Adding the same natural number to every block raises the index by a multiple of $C$ and eventually makes all weights nonnegative. This is Wilson's classical large-index theorem, cited as reference 6 by Graham, Li and Li. Erdős 722 instead fixes the index at one and lets the order grow. The checked equivalence `eventual_positivity_iff` identifies exactly the remaining positivity assertion in that quantifier order. [Large-index spectrum](Erdos722AllRankIndices.lean) · [Host equivalence](Erdos722SteinerWeights.lean) · [Graham, Li and Li, *On the structure of t-designs*](https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~ronspubs/80_02_t_designs.pdf)
