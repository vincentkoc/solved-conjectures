# The spectrum of Steiner quadruple systems

For every integer $n\ge 4$, a family on $n$ points satisfies the upstream predicate `H.IsBlockDesign 3 4` exactly when $n\equiv 2,4\pmod 6$. The proof also classifies the small orders. The empty family qualifies on zero, one and two points, and no family qualifies on three. Thus the pinned divisibility hypotheses suffice at every $n\ge 4$ for $r=3$, $k=4$. Hanani proved this spectrum in 1960. The general Erdős 722 declaration quantifies over all $1\le r<k$. ## Four columns and two shared points

Take an SQS of order $v=2m+2$ with $m\ge 1$. Keep two points as a common stem. Give each of the four copies its own column of $2m$ ordinary points. The new order is $8m+2=4v-6$. Split the columns into two halves with two columns apiece. Write $h,i\in\mathbb F_2$ for the half and column, and label ordinary points by $(a,u)\in\mathbb F_2\times\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z$. A triple contained in one column and its stem uses the source SQS. [Fourfold recurrence](Erdos722QuadrupleSpectrum.lean) · [Construction 5 in Sage](https://github.com/sagemath/sage/blob/c7cb3ca95a456a64ddc1364f1f4a89212354f522/src/sage/combinat/designs/steiner_quadruple_systems.py#L291)

Two points in different columns of half $h$, with labels $(a,u)$ and $(b,v')$, select an edge colour in column $j$ of the opposite half. Identify a label $(d,w)$ there with $d m+w$ modulo $2m$, using the least representative of $w$. Pair vertices whose sum is $2(u+v')+e$ modulo $2m$, where $e=a+b+j$ is a bit. An odd sum pairs every vertex. An even sum leaves precisely $(0,u+v')$ and $(1,u+v')$ uncovered. As $j$ varies, exactly one column has an even sum. For a hole $(d,u+v')$, use the shared point $h+a+d+ij$ as its mate, where $i$ is the column containing $(a,u)$. Choosing the other point of the pair gives the same stem label. Reversing the two halves exchanges the stem labels. [Cyclic and round-robin colourings](Erdos722FourfoldColours.lean) · [Hole characterization and crossing pairs](Erdos722FourfoldBridge.lean)

Within a half, two points in one column and a third in its partner column use the same colour in a one-factorization of both complete graphs. For a fixed pair in opposite halves, the row of the cyclic edge colouring is a bijection. Replacing its would-be repeated endpoint by the prescribed stem makes the completion an involution. The two orientations use different stems, and both filled columns agree on repeated points. These identities give one four-set through each triple. [Symmetry and embedded columns](Erdos722FourfoldCompletion.lean) · [Cancellation and the complete rule](Erdos722FourfoldRule.lean)

## From the recurrence to the spectrum

The notebook's earlier constructions reduced sufficiency for every admissible order to the step $v\mapsto4v-6$. The new recurrence discharges that last hypothesis. For necessity, the existing incidence count gives $\binom{4-i}{3-i}\mid\binom{n-i}{3-i}$ for $0\le i<3$ whenever $n\ge 4$. The count at $i=2$ also excludes $n=3$.
