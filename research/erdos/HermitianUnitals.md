# Hermitian unitals on the lines of projective space

For every prime power $q$ and every $t\ge1$, there is a Steiner pair design with block size $q+1$ on
$$v_t=1+q^3+(q^3)^2+\cdots+(q^3)^t$$
points. At $q=4$ the first three orders are $65$, $4161$ and $266305$, all at block size five. The proof constructs the classical Hermitian unital and fills each line of a projective space with a copy. [Hermitian curve proof](Erdos722HermitianUnitals.lean) · [Projective construction](Erdos722ProjectiveUnitals.lean)

## The seed and the extension

Write $E=\mathbf F_{q^2}$ and $F=\mathbf F_q$. The vertex set consists of one point at infinity and the affine pairs satisfying $\operatorname{Tr}(y)=N(x)$. The trace has fibers of size $q$, giving $q^3+1$ vertices. Every vertical line contains $q$ affine points and the point at infinity. On the line $y=mx+b$ the equation becomes
$$N(x-m^q)=\operatorname{Tr}(b)+N(m).$$
A zero right side gives one point. Each nonzero right side gives $q+1$ points by surjectivity of the norm on units. Two distinct curve points determine one line, and that line is a secant. This proves the classical Hermitian unital parameters in both odd and even characteristic. [Thas, introduction](https://ftp.gwdg.de/pub/EMIS/journals/JACO/Volume1_1/l120232652xwv113.fulltext.pdf)

A projective line over $\mathbf F_{q^3}$ has $q^3+1$ points. Label each line of $\mathrm{PG}(t,q^3)$ by the vertices of one unital, and include the images of its secant blocks. A pair of global points first determines its projective line, then its block in that line's unital. The Lean lemma `fill_lines` proves this replacement for arbitrary line embeddings and any Steiner pair design on their common domain. The proof uses mathlib's projective point counts and line uniqueness. [Dukes and Ling, Construction 2.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1401.1471) · [Point counts](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/0df444a360eaa60ab8c11dca51a86af692955474/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Projectivization/Cardinality.lean) · [Line uniqueness](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/0df444a360eaa60ab8c11dca51a86af692955474/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Projectivization/Collinear.lean)
