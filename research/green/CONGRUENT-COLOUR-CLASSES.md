# A square-root obstruction for congruent colour classes

For each $n=2^m$, the binary cube of dimension $n$ admits a partition into $2n$ nonempty translates of one set $T$. Every class has the same twofold sum $T+T$. Every affine subspace in that sum has codimension at least $\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor$. In particular the logarithmic bound asked about in Green’s discussion of problem 53 fails even for these partitions. The existence of some bound depending only on the number of colours remains open. [Green’s problems 52 and 53](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/greenbj/papers/open-problems.pdf#page=26) · [Partition proof](Green53CongruentColours.lean)

## From a Hamming ball to a partition

Index the $n$ coordinates by $\mathbb F_2^m$. The affine binary words form a linear code $H$ of order $2n$. For any word, the mean square of its Walsh correlations with the linear words is $n$. Choosing a correlation of large magnitude and allowing the constant bit therefore gives a codeword within Hamming distance $\lfloor(n-\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor)/2\rfloor$. Green credits Kaave Hosseini and Ryan Alweiss with the Hamming-ball counterexample to the logarithmic version of problem 52. KitaKen1’s existing Lean proof already gives Walsh centres for that counterexample. [Green’s 2025 update](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/greenbj/papers/open-problems.pdf#page=26) · [Earlier Lean proof](https://github.com/KitaKen1/green-52-log-counterexample/blob/16cb5d0/lean/Green52LogCounterexampleFC.lean)

The ball meets every coset of $H$. Pick one point from each coset to form $T$. The translates $T+h$ for $h\in H$ partition the cube, and $h+h=0$ makes all their twofold sums equal. Every word in $T+T$ has weight at most $n-\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor$. A flat of dimension $r$ projects onto all binary values in some $r$ coordinates, so it contains a word of weight at least $r$. This proves the asserted codimension bound. [Walsh and transversal proof](Green52WalshTiles.lean) · [Congruent partitions](Green53CongruentColours.lean)

## Arbitrary colour counts and quadratic transversals

For **every** $K\ge8$, any codimension bound $c$ satisfying either pinned problem obeys $K<4(c(K)+1)^2$. Enlarge a dyadic complement to size $K$ while retaining its cover. Green’s partition-to-cover implication transfers the bound at that same $K$. No monotonicity assumption on $c$ is needed. The congruent partitions above witness the sharper dyadic result directly. [Pinned Green 52](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/blob/f606af160664c5a10952e9a957b100a1cd45bbeb/FormalConjectures/GreensOpenProblems/52.lean) · [Pinned Green 53](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/blob/f606af160664c5a10952e9a957b100a1cd45bbeb/FormalConjectures/GreensOpenProblems/53.lean)

The tile is itself a full function graph with $m+1$ output coordinates. If a translate contains a quadratic graph in any complementary coordinates, the earlier quadratic-flat theorem forces its output dimension $d$ to satisfy $d^2\ge\lfloor\sqrt{2^m}\rfloor$. For example, at $m=20$ the tile has 21 output coordinates, while any such quadratic graph needs at least 32.
