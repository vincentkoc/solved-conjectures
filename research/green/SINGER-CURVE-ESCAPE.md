# Bounded-degree curves in the opposite Singer layer

Let \(q\) be the field order and \(v=q^2+q+1\). Write an ordinary cyclic basis as \(T=2E\cup(1+2B)\subseteq\mathbb Z/(2v)\), with \(T+T=\mathbb Z/(2v)\). Suppose \(q\to\infty\), \(|T|/\sqrt{2v}\to\sqrt2\), and a cubic Singer line \(D\subseteq\mathbb Z/v\) satisfies \(|D\setminus E|=o(q)\). For each fixed \(d\), choose coordinates on the cubic extension and a nonzero bivariate polynomial \(f\) of degree at most \(d\) in each variable. Both may vary with the field. Then
\[
\big|\{b\in B:\ -b\text{ labels an affine zero of } f
                 \text{ or a point at infinity}\}\big|=o(q).
\]
In fact \(|B|/q\to1\), so the fraction of the opposite layer on the curve tends to zero. Additional even points and all odd points are arbitrary. The result includes reducible curves, repeated components, and the entire line at infinity. A projective curve of bounded degree fits by dehomogenizing its nonzero homogeneous equation. The Lean statement takes the affine polynomial and includes all infinity points explicitly. ## Finite bound

First take \(R=2D\cup(1+2B)\) with \(|D|=q+1\), \(|B|\le q+1\) and \(H=2v-|R+R|\). Put \(w=1+d_x+d_y\), where the coefficients of \(f\) as a polynomial in \(Y\) have \(X\)-degree at most \(d_x\), and \(\deg_Y f\le d_y\). Let \(V\) be the opposite points on the affine curve or at infinity. If \(H\le q^2/16\) and the reflected seed at each exceptional line center misses at least \(q^2/8\) doubled residues, then
\[
  |V|/q\ \le\ 1/q+\sqrt{w/q+wH/q^2}+w/q+64wH/q^2 .
\]
Consequently \(w=o(q)\) and \(wH=o(q^2)\) suffice even for growing degrees. The reflected condition holds eventually, uniformly in the center, since every choice of reflection axes has limiting doubled coverage \(7/8\). [Finite estimates](Green33SingerCurveEscape.lean) · [Degree-dependent limit](Green33SingerCurveSupport.lean)

To see the two contributions, remove all affine line components and the line at infinity. Every projective line meets the residual set \(C\subseteq B\) in at most \(d_x+d_y\) points. Unique differences on \(D\) and the count of repeated mixed sums give \(|C|(|C|-1)\le(d_x+d_y)(q+H)\). For an exceptional line with \(m\) opposite points and reflected deficit \(H_r\), secant counts and the effect of changing the seed give \((H_r-H)(m-1)\le2(q+1)H\) whenever \(m>1\). There are at most \(d_x\) vertical components, \(d_y\) other affine line components, and one line at infinity. Vertical components vanish on the leading coefficient in \(Y\). The other components are distinct roots \(mX+b\) in the integral domain \(\mathbb F_q[X]\). [Polynomial lemmas](Green33AffineCurves.lean) · [Singer coordinates and sign convention](Green33SingerCurveChart.lean)

For a general full basis the odd sums force \(|E||B|\ge v\), while \(|E|+|B|=(2+o(1))q\). Both parity classes therefore have size \((1+o(1))q\). Trim \(B\) to at most \(q+1\) points and replace \(E\) by \(D\). Only \(o(q)\) original points are lost, costing at most \(o(q^2)\) represented residues. This transfers the curve estimate to the original basis.
