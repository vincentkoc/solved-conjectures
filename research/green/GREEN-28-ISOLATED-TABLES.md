# Five-site pieces of an integer sum in Green 28

Let $P,Q$ be independent integer laws whose sum is uniform on its support. If **each marginal has at most five occupied sites**, then both marginals are uniform. The integer gaps and positive real masses are arbitrary. The theorem also applies when both supports are arbitrarily large, provided each has a nonempty isolated piece of at most five sites, defined below. The general conjecture remains open. [Five-site theorem](Green28FiveSiteCertificate.lean) · [Isolated-piece theorem](Green28IsolatedTables.lean) · [Pinned Green 28](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/blob/f606af160664c5a10952e9a957b100a1cd45bbeb/FormalConjectures/GreensOpenProblems/28.lean)

## Conditioning on complete sum representations

Write $A,B$ for the supports. A set of rows $S\subseteq A$ is closed if every representation of a value in $S+B$ uses a row in $S$. Equivalently, $S+B$ is disjoint from $(A\setminus S)+B$. Columns are defined by interchanging the sides. The two isolation hypotheses use the **full** opposite supports. A single sum with a unique representation at an endpoint does not establish either condition.

For a nonempty closed $S$, condition $P$ on $S$. Every occupied sum of the conditioned law receives its entire original mass, multiplied by the same factor $1/P(S)$. Thus the new independent sum is uniform. A second closed set of columns $T$ can then be conditioned in the same way. If a theorem forces either conditional marginal to be uniform on this rectangle, apply the rational-ratio criterion to that marginal paired with the full opposite law. This makes the full opposite law uniform. A second use of the criterion gives uniformity of the original pair. Applying the five-site theorem on $S,T$ proves the stated result. [Restriction and rectangle transfer](Green28IsolatedTables.lean) · [Rational-ratio criterion](GREEN-28-ARITHMETIC.md)

For example, take sets $A_0,B_0$ of at most five nonnegative sites with $\max A_0+\max B_0<L$. Put $A=A_0+L\{0,\ldots,u-1\}$ and $B=B_0+Lu\{0,\ldots,v-1\}$ for positive integers $u,v$. In every occupied sum, the quotient on division by $L$ uniquely specifies the two block indices. Thus any one block on each side qualifies, independently of $u,v$ and of the masses within or across blocks.

Join two occupied rows when they participate in representations of the same sum. The absence of a proper nonempty closed subset says exactly that this finite graph is connected. If a counterexample has disconnected rows or columns, conditioning on a proper closed piece reduces the total number of sites and preserves a counterexample. Induction proves that the **full pinned statement is equivalent to its restriction to pairs connected on both sides**. The Lean statement spells out the original finite-support hypotheses and uses the original sum and uniformity predicates. [`connected_iff`](Green28IsolatedTables.lean)

## The five-site certificate

In the ordered sum table let $c$ be the mass of each occupied output. A pair contributes at most $c$, with equality exactly at a uniquely represented sum. Both extreme sums are unique, and comparison with the first row and column shows that the singleton cells are precisely the rectangle of maximal rows and maximal columns. In particular, both end rows and end columns are maximal. It suffices to rule out a rectangle missing at least one row and one column. The existing four-site proof supplies the probability argument and the smaller tables. [Probability argument](Green28FourAtomLaws.lean) · [Four-site classification](GREEN-28-FOUR-ATOMS.md)

After transposing as needed, the remaining tables have five columns and three, four or five rows. The possible nonempty sets of deficient interior sites give $1\cdot7+3\cdot7+7\cdot7=77$ cases. A deficient cell $(i,j)$ requires another deficient cell $(k,l)$ with $a_i+b_j=a_k+b_l$. Strict order means that one index increases and the other decreases. A cell in the maximal rectangle forbids every other representation of its sum.

The certificate records which deficient cell to split on, or which maximal cell would acquire an extra representation. At each split Lean enumerates **all** crossing partners. Each terminal implication follows by integer linear arithmetic from the strict site orders and the equalities accumulated on that branch. The 77 trees contain 1,041 instructions. The case in which all six interior sites are deficient uses 34 splits. Z3 selected the branches during the search. Replaying the proof needs only Lean and the pinned imports. There is no coordinate cutoff. [Certificate data, replay and grid theorem](Green28FiveSiteCertificate.lean)
