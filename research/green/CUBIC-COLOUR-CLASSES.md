# Common differences in cubic colour classes

Let $f:\mathbb F_2^n\to\mathbb F_2^d$ have vanishing fourth mixed differences. There is one nonempty affine flat $W$, of codimension bounded only by $d$, on which every occupied fibre of $f$ has many pairs. For each $z\in W$ and each attained $y$, at least $2^{n-5d}$ inputs $x$ satisfy $f(x)=f(x+z)=y$. This proves Green 53 for partitions obtained by assigning arbitrary colours to the values of $f$. Each nonempty colour class contains an entire fibre, so the same $W$ and representation bound apply after regrouping. [Lean proof](Green53PolynomialColours.lean) · [Pinned Green 53](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/blob/f606af160664c5a10952e9a957b100a1cd45bbeb/FormalConjectures/GreensOpenProblems/53.lean)

**Finding the common flat.** A nonzero Boolean function of additive degree at most $e$ has a set of at most $2^e$ shifts that meets its support from every starting point. To see this, take a nonzero first difference and apply induction. At a point where the difference is nonzero, one of its two endpoints lies in the original support. The indicator of a single cubic fibre is a product of $d$ Boolean factors and has degree at most $3d$. Take the union $S$ of the shift sets for all occupied fibres. Then $|S|\le 2^d 2^{3d}$. [Hitting shifts and fibre indicators](Green53PolynomialColours.lean)

The tuple $x\mapsto(f(x+s))_{s\in S}$ is still cubic. The horizontal theorem gives a flat of directions $z$ for which two such tuples at $x$ and $x+z$ agree. For each fibre, one of the matching coordinates lands in it. Writing $H$ for the horizontal codimension bound, this gives codimension at most $H((2^d)^{2^{4d}})$. For a single prescribed fibre the exponent $2^{4d}$ improves to $2^{3d}$. [Cubic horizontal theorem and explicit bound](CUBIC-TRANSVERSALS.md)

**Counting representations.** For any fixed displacement $z$ and values $u,v$, the pair conditions are $f(x)=u$ and $f(x+z)-f(x)=v-u$. These have $d$ scalar equations of degree at most three and $d$ of degree at most two. Their joint indicator has degree at most $5d$. Counting translates in the hitting-shift lemma gives the classical Reed–Muller minimum-distance bound. Every realized pair of fibre values therefore occurs on at least a $2^{-5d}$ fraction of inputs. [Lean counting lemma](Green53PolynomialColours.lean) · [Reed’s binary codes](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.1954.1057465)

The restriction concerns the algebraic description of the colours. Green 53 remains open for arbitrary partitions. Jaber and Kumar treat different special cases involving Hamming balls. [Jaber and Kumar](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07918) · [Replay instructions](../README.md#replay)
